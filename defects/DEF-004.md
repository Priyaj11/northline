# DEF-004  Page-level accessibility failures on every page

Status:          Open
Severity:        Medium
Priority:        P3
Requirement:     REQ-ACC-001
Test cases:      TC-ACC-001, TC-ACC-002, TC-ACC-003
Detected in:     Phase 5A, automated accessibility scan
Reproducibility: Consistent, every scan

## Environment

  System Under Test: ParaBank, image parasoft/parabank
  Environment:       local
  Tool:              axe-core 4.13.0, rule tags wcag2a wcag2aa wcag21a wcag21aa
  Browser:           Chromium
  Detected:          2026-08-31

## Summary

Four accessibility rules fail on all five scanned pages.

    rule              impact    success criteria   description
    ---------------   -------   ----------------   -------------------------------
    html-has-lang     serious   WCAG 3.1.1         no lang attribute on <html>
    image-alt         critical  WCAG 1.1.1         image with no alternative text
    link-name         serious   WCAG 2.4.4, 4.1.2  link with no discernible text
    color-contrast    serious   WCAG 1.4.3         contrast below the AA threshold

Colour contrast affects the most elements: 6 on login, 14 on registration, 4 on
the overview, 3 on transfer, 13 on bill payment.

## Steps to reproduce

  1. .venv/bin/python scripts/scan_accessibility.py
  2. Read docs/accessibility-report.md

## Expected result

No critical or serious violations, as stated by REQ-ACC-001.

## Actual result

Totals across the five scanned pages: 11 critical, 15 serious.

## Business impact

A missing lang attribute means a screen reader may announce the page in the
wrong language, which can render it unintelligible. A link with no discernible
text is announced as "link" with no indication of where it goes. Insufficient
contrast affects anyone with low vision, and in practice anyone reading a
screen in bright light.

None of these blocks a task outright, which is why this is Medium rather than
High. DEF-003 covers the failures that do block tasks.

## Note on the severity split

These findings were separated from DEF-003 deliberately rather than filed as
one large accessibility defect. They have different impacts, different fixes,
and different urgencies. A single defect saying "26 accessibility violations"
gives a team no way to prioritise, and the naming failures in DEF-003 need
fixing well before the contrast ratios here.
