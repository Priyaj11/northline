## TC-XFER-001 Transfer a typical valid amount between two of the customer's accounts

**Requirement** REQ-XFER-001  
**Technique** Equivalence Partitioning  
**Layer** ui  
**Priority** Critical  
**Automation** planned  
**Introduced in phase** 3

**Preconditions** The customer owns at least two accounts and the source holds at least 100.00.

**Test data** amount: 100.00, source and destination read at runtime

**Steps**

1. Log in and open the transfer page.
2. Enter the amount and choose a source and a destination account.
3. Submit the transfer.

**Expected result** A confirmation is displayed naming the amount and both accounts.

## TC-XFER-003 The source is debited and the destination credited by the same amount

**Requirement** REQ-XFER-002  
**Technique** Equivalence Partitioning  
**Layer** database  
**Priority** Critical  
**Automation** planned  
**Introduced in phase** 4

**Preconditions** Two accounts are known and their balances have been recorded.

**Test data** amount: 100.00

**Steps**

1. Record both balances before the transfer.
2. Perform the transfer.
3. Record both balances after the transfer.
4. Compare the differences against the amount.

**Expected result** The source balance falls by exactly the amount and the destination rises by exactly the amount. No money is created or lost. Asserted on the difference rather than the absolute balance, so the test survives any starting state.

## TC-XFER-015 Establish how a transfer exceeding the available balance is handled

**Requirement** REQ-XFER-007  
**Technique** Boundary Value Analysis  
**Layer** api  
**Priority** High  
**Automation** planned  
**Introduced in phase** 4

**Preconditions** The source account balance is known.

**Test data** amounts at balance minus 0.01, exactly balance, and balance plus 0.01

**Steps**

1. Read the source balance.
2. Attempt a transfer of the balance minus one cent.
3. Attempt a transfer of exactly the balance.
4. Attempt a transfer of the balance plus one cent.
5. Read the balances after each attempt.

**Expected result** The first two succeed. The behaviour of the third is unconfirmed, which is why REQ-XFER-007 is marked assumed. Whatever it does, the debit must equal the credit. This case exists to establish the actual rule, and the requirement register will be corrected to match rather than a defect being invented.
